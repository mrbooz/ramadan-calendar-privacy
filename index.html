RAMADAN CALENDAR

Privacy Policy

Ramadan Calendar & Prayer · Android · mrbooz AB · Last updated 24 November 2026

Short version: nothing about you leaves your phone. Ramadan Calendar has no account, no ads and no tracking. Your data stays on your phone. The app sends anonymous usage counts (for example "the Quran tab was opened") so we can see which parts are used. They cannot be linked to you or your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Your city or approximate location, used to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction.
  • Your settings, prayer log, fasting log, goals, tasbih counts, bookmarks and saved duas.

This data is stored only on your device and is never sent to us. If your phone's Android backup is on, Android may include it in your own Google backup; we cannot read that backup.

Anonymous usage counts

From version 1.0.3 the app sends short anonymous messages to our server when you open a screen, start or finish setup, open the Plus page, tap a buy button, complete a purchase or share something. Once a day it also sends the app version, the app language, the country of the city you picked, and whether Plus, prayer alerts and the home-screen widget are on.

These messages contain no name, account, advertising ID, device ID or other identifier, and no location or times. The server does not keep IP addresses or request logs. It only adds one to a daily total (for example "Quran tab opened: 120 times on 1 March"), so no record exists of what any one phone did. The totals are stored in Google Cloud (Firestore, Europe), are not shared or sold, and are used only to improve the app.

Location

If you allow it, the app asks Android for your approximate location once to find your city. The location is used on the phone to calculate times and is never sent to us or anyone else. You can choose a city by hand instead.

Purchases

Ramadan Plus is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment and tells the app whether Plus is active. We do not receive your name, email or payment details. Google's own privacy policy applies to the purchase: policies.google.com/privacy.

Sharing

When you share a card, dua or verse, the app hands it to the app you pick (for example a messenger). Nothing is shared unless you tap Share.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone. The anonymous counts above are not personal data.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings. That removes everything on your phone. The anonymous daily totals cannot be traced to you, so there is nothing of yours on our server to delete.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above. Questions: hello@mrbooz.com · mrbooz AB, Sweden.
