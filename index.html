RAMADAN CALENDAR

Privacy Policy

Ramadan Calendar & Prayer · Android · mrbooz AB · Last updated 19 November 2026

Short version: we collect nothing. Ramadan Calendar has no account, no ads and no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Your city or approximate location, used to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction.
  • Your settings, prayer log, fasting log, goals, tasbih counts, bookmarks and saved duas.

This data is stored only on your device. We have no server that receives it. If your phone's Android backup is on, Android may include it in your own Google backup; we cannot read that backup.

Location

If you allow it, the app asks Android for your approximate location once to find your city. The location is used on the phone to calculate times and is never sent to us or anyone else. You can choose a city by hand instead.

Purchases

Ramadan Plus is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment and tells the app whether Plus is active. We do not receive your name, email or payment details. Google's own privacy policy applies to the purchase: policies.google.com/privacy.

Sharing

When you share a card, dua or verse, the app hands it to the app you pick (for example a messenger). Nothing is shared unless you tap Share.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings. That removes everything.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above. Questions: hello@mrbooz.com · mrbooz AB, Sweden.
